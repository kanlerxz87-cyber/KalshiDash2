Volume × price proxy is a project-defined proxy: volumefp × last_price_dollars per market, summed over the event. It is not actual dollars traded; Kalshi documents volume as contracts and last_price as the last traded YES price. Both inputs come from Get Market fields ↗.

Dollars actually traded — official GET /markets/trades

The column above is a proxy: cumulative contracts × the current last YES price. This panel computes the real figure from the trades the exchange reports, Σ(count_fp × the price of the side the taker bought), so it is dollars that changed hands in the window — not an estimate. It costs one request per market, so it runs only when you press the button, and it measures the highest-volume markets rather than every market. The count of markets measured, and those skipped, is printed with the result.

Σ(count_fp × yes_price_dollars for a YES taker, no_price_dollars for a NO taker), from Get Trades ↗. min_ts/max_ts are Unix seconds, as the reference requires. If any page of trades cannot be read, the event shows n/a and the topic total is withheld — a partial sum is never presented as a total. A trade with no recorded taker side, or no price on the side taken, is excluded and counted instead of being valued by assumption; each market measured carries its own response link, so every row can be checked.
