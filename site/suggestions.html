Suggestions for better decisions

Proposals to improve how this dashboard supports decisions. Nothing here is implemented. Each suggestion states the decision it improves, the evidence behind it, its cost and risk, and the information still missing before it could be chosen. Pick a path; the site will not pick one for you.

How to choose

The order below is deliberate: items that make the data trustworthy come before items that make it richer, which come before items that make it prettier. A suggestion is only actionable when its "missing information" is closed — those are cross-referenced to the questions page.

The four that matter most

S1 — Prove one real order end to end

Everything about trading is currently inferred from documentation. One deliberate 1-contract demo order, followed by a fill appearing in /portfolio/fills, converts the largest block of the site from unverified to verified. Nothing else on this page improves trust as much for as little effort.

S3 — Pair fills into round trips

Premium paid is not performance. Pairing each fill into an entry and exit (or holding to settlement) is what turns the Strategies page from a catalogue into a scoreboard. It needs fills first, so it follows S1.

S5 — Publish a data-freshness clock

REST quotes are cached for a few seconds, and the demo exchange is not production. Showing how stale each number is prevents a viewer from acting on a price that moved. Cheap, and it removes a real misreading risk.

S8 — Resolve the multi-session overlap

Seven branches wrote to this repository concurrently. Until one owner performs the integration, any page may be replaced by another session's work. This is an operational risk to everything else on this list.
