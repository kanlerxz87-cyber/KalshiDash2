Kalshi dashboard: sourced, flagged, and sample-based

Topics: gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf. Data captured 2026-11-20 from official Kalshi API responses and EIA tables. Every row links to its source.

Trading and portfolio are blocked. No Kalshi demo API key was available, and the build sandbox cannot reach Kalshi directly. No trades were placed and no portfolio data was pulled. See Portfolio.

Executive summary

  • What was collected. 21 Kalshi series, 9 captured markets (2 finalized), 8 EIA reference prices, and 21 flagged items. Each row has a source link.
  • Gas. The two Kalshi diesel series (DIESEL, DIESELM) returned no open markets. The DIESEL contract terms name a different EIA table from the one the API lists as its settlement source (F-02). No retail gasoline series was found in the tag query (F-03). EIA's latest U.S. retail diesel is $5.937/gal (week of 11/16/26).
  • Crypto. The two most recent finalized monthly Bitcoin above-$100k markets in the sample settled NO. The open November and December markets show last prices of $0.01 and $0.02. The November market's data may be stale (F-09).
  • Golf. Three 2nd-round PGA 3-ball markets show two-sided quotes but zero volume (F-13). One golf series' only settlement source is a participant's own YouTube channel (F-15).
  • Elections. Two NATO Secretary General markets (Kallas, Iohannis) were captured with low volume. One election series has placeholder text as its title (F-05). Another is titled only "LAR" (F-06).
  • Culture. Both culture series returned no markets. Their settlement lists include placeholder text and Google search URLs (F-07, F-08). Treat these as unverified.
  • Measure. The API reports contract counts, not dollar volume (F-17). Rankings here use contract counts. Dollar-amount ranking needs a decision (see Suggestions).
  • Live. The live dashboard fetches the public Kalshi API in the browser. Whether that works from GitHub Pages is unverified (F-18).
