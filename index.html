Highest-dollar live markets, tracked to resolution

A read-only-to-trade dashboard for the Kalshi demo exchange covering five topics: gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf. It discovers markets from the live API, ranks them by money traded, follows each one until the exchange reports a yes or no result, and charts contract price against time.
