Highest-dollar live markets, tracked to resolution

A read-only-to-trade dashboard for the Kalshi demo exchange covering five topics: gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf. It discovers markets from the live API, ranks them by money traded, follows each one until the exchange reports a yes or no result, and charts contract price against time.

Connecting to the Kalshi demo API…

Public market data is read without credentials from https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2. If this banner stays, the browser could not reach the API — no values are shown in that case.

Live events by topic

Open events with at least one market, ranked by money traded (volume × last price). Click a topic for the full table, chart and order-book depth.
