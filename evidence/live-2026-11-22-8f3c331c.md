```json

 "contract_terms_url":"https://assets.kalshi.com/contract_terms/GAS.pdf",
 "contract_url":"https://assets.kalshi.com/regulatory/product-certifications/GAS.pdf",
 "exchange_index":0,"fee_multiplier":1,"fee_type":"quadratic","frequency":"weekly",
 "last_updated_ts":"2026-11-02T23:01:12.873951Z",
 "settlement_sources":[{"name":"U.S. Energy Information Agency",
   "url":"https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/PET_PRI_GND_DCUS_NUS_W.htm"}],
 "tags":null,"ticker":"GAS","title":"Gas weekly"}}
```

Three findings, in order of how much they matter here:

1. **Flagged X1 is reproducible on the live exchange today, not only in the committed snapshot**: the
   `contract_url` for `KXGAS` points at `kalshi-public-docs-staging.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com` while its
   sibling `contract_terms_url` points at `assets.kalshi.com`. `GAS`, the other record with the same title,
   has the production host for both. No page renders these fields; the demo API serves them.
2. **`GET /series/KXGAS` returns `ticker: "KXGAS"`.** The collector comment asserting it resolves to a series
   whose ticker is `GAS` is not reproducible and has been reworded (§7 of the report).
3. **A series record carries its own `settlement_sources`.** `assets/js/market.js` now keeps them and the
   category pages list them as the source of the topic's underlying number — here, the EIA weekly retail
   gasoline series. Not a claimed cause of any price move.

   {"description":"Default","exchange_active":true,"exchange_index":0,"intra_exchange_transfers_active":true,"trading_active":true},
   {"description":"Demo shard 1","exchange_active":true,"exchange_index":1,"intra_exchange_transfers_active":true,"trading_active":true},

exchange-baseline alert (Flagged Z1, W3).
