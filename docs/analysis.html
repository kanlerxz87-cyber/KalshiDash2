Everything here is computed from the local trading journal — the orders this site actually submitted from this browser — joined to the demo exchange's own market data. No sample trades, no modelled P&L, no projections. When the journal is empty, the page says so and shows nothing.

Why the page can be empty while your account has trades. Orders placed in the Kalshi web UI, or from another browser, are not in this journal. Load them here from Portfolio → Read orders, or use Import account data below.
